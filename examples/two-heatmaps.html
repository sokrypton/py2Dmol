Two heatmaps, one panel

Both maps below are computed from the structure that is drawn, so nothing here is invented: Contact is 1 / (1 + exp((d - 8 Å) / 1.5)) over Cα–Cα distances, and Cα–Cα distance is that distance in Ångstrom over 0–127.5. Each map states its own vmin, vmax and colors, so neither depends on what the panel happens to know about its key — contact has a built-in scale and this overrides it, distance has none and needs no entry. A map's tab is named by its KEY (shown even when there is only one) and xlabel/ylabel caption the axes — pae defaults to “Scored position” / “Aligned position”. Pick between them with the tabs in the corner of the plot. Drag a box on either one — a box is stored in residues, so it means the same thing on both, and switching tabs keeps it.
